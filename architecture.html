01. Executive Summary & The Two-Loop Architecture

Smallholder farmers across rural India face severe infrastructure constraints: intermittent or nonexistent cellular connectivity in remote farm parcels, high ambient thermal stress, budget smartphone hardware, and diverse regional dialects. Concurrently, agricultural authorities grapple with delayed epidemic visibility, often relying on manual paper-based field surveys that register pest infestations and fungal blights weeks after spore vectors cross taluka boundaries.

KrishiRakshak bridges this operational chasm by enforcing a clean architectural bifurcation into The Two Operational Loops:

⚡ The Fast Local Loop (Agrarian Edge Sovereignty)

Image capture, hardware EXIF canonicalization, spatial leaf isolation (YOLOv8n), fine-grained pathological classification (EfficientNet-Lite0), and Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC) knapsack spray satchel calculations execute 100% locally on the farmer's smartphone in milliseconds with zero internet connectivity.

Field reports persist locally inside WatermelonDB (SQLite) using client-generated v4 UUIDs and sync opportunistically when connectivity resumes. Central PostgreSQL/PostGIS database RPCs synthesize these reports into 10 km spatial outbreak clusters, while our scientific backend couples multispectral Sentinel-2 NDVI satellite layers with atmospheric Gaussian plume dispersion math to project downwind pathogen vectors and dispatch targeted DLT SMS advisories.

🧠 Stage 1: Spatial Leaf Localization (YOLOv8n INT8)

An INT8 quantized YOLOv8 nano model takes a 640×640 input and isolates the dominant agricultural leaf bounding box, stripping away background weeds, dry soil, and non-foliar artifacts.

🔬 Stage 2: Fine-Grained Pathogen Classification (EfficientNet-Lite0 INT8)

The cropped leaf sub-tensor is bilinear-interpolated to 224×224 and fed into an INT8 quantized EfficientNet-Lite0 backbone, resolving subtle lesion textures, foliar spots, and chlorosis across 40 agricultural classes.

05. Deterministic Preprocessing: Hardware EXIF Canonicalization

A fundamental challenge in edge computer vision is sensor orientation variance: depending on the handset manufacturer and camera driver, raw image buffers are captured rotated by 90°, 180°, or 270° with an EXIF orientation metadata flag.

📐 The 3.4-Million-Parameter Conservation Principle

Rather than forcing EfficientNet-Lite0's 3.4 million parameters to expend neural capacity learning rotation invariance via expensive data augmentation, KrishiRakshak applies a zero-cost deterministic EXIF canonicalization rule in native Android hardware before memory buffer allocation.

By handling orientation deterministically in the hardware camera pipeline, 100% of EfficientNet's depthwise separable convolution kernels remain dedicated to extracting pathological fungal, bacterial, and viral feature representations.

06. Domain Adaptation & Agronomic Guardrails

6.1 Synthetic Field Augmentation & Laboratory Bias Elimination

Public agricultural datasets such as PlantVillage suffer from notorious laboratory background bias: leaves are photographed against flat white, gray, or solid black laboratory backdrops. When raw models trained on such datasets encounter real Indian fields, they suffer catastrophic domain collapse.

To bridge this domain gap, we engineered an automated pipeline that segments the leaf silhouette and composites it onto rich, realistic agronomic backgrounds—including damp soil textures, mulched rows, and surrounding canopy foliage. This regularizes spatial gradients and forces the model to key off genuine lesion necrosis rather than sterile lab artifacts.

6.2 Logit Masking for Biologically Similar Crops (Poaceae Family Guardrail)

Certain crops share close botanical lineage—such as Corn (*Zea mays*) and Sugarcane (*Saccharum officinarum*), both members of the Poaceae (grass) family. Under low-light conditions, raw convolutional feature maps can exhibit cross-crop confusion.

KrishiRakshak enforces an explicit software guardrail:

  • Softmax Logit Masking: When a farmer selects a specific crop cohort (e.g. Corn), logits corresponding to other crop families are mathematically masked prior to the softmax probability distribution calculation.
  • Pre-Masking Consistency Check: If the unmasked model logits strongly signal a different crop with high certainty before masking occurs, the app gracefully prompts the farmer to verify their selection, preventing accidental misclassification while respecting user agency.

Data persistence at the edge is powered by WatermelonDB sitting directly on native Android SQLite. WatermelonDB provides a fully observable, lazy-loaded local database that scales to hundreds of thousands of records without memory leaks or UI thread stutter.

7.3 Opportunistic Outbox Sync Worker

When network connectivity flickers back, an outbox background worker performs idempotent batch upserts to Supabase PostgreSQL (ON CONFLICT (id) DO UPDATE) and transfers high-resolution JPEGs to the cloud storage bucket without locking the farmer's UI. If transmission drops mid-flight, zero data is lost; writes simply pause and resume on the next connectivity window.

11.2 High-Precision HEXEWKB Spatial Coordinate Decoding

PostGIS stores geographic points as 50-character Hexadecimal Extended Well-Known Binary (HEXEWKB) strings using the WGS 84 spatial reference identifier (SRID 4326): 

0101000020E6100000 + [16 Hex chars: Float64 Longitude] + [16 Hex chars: Float64 Latitude]

Both our Node.js and Python backend engines implement high-speed binary IEEE 754 decoding to parse native float coordinates in sub-microsecond time without relying on heavy external GIS abstractions.

11.3 Verification Lifecycle Trigger (trg_field_report_triage)

To maintain data integrity and enforce verification workflows, a database trigger executes BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE on public.field_reports:

  • Auto-Validation: If a report is inserted with high AI confidence ($P \ge 0.70$), it defaults to verification_status = 'auto_validated'.
  • Pending Triage: Scans with $P < 0.70$ or flagged anomalies enter verification_status = 'pending', routing to extension workers.
  • Expert Audit Immutability: When an agronomist reviews a report, expert_diagnosis and expert_id are written, but the original ai_diagnosis is never altered, guaranteeing an immutable audit trail for model evaluation.

15. Epidemiological Spread Modeling & Mathematical Physics

Biological outbreaks do not disperse in uniform radial circles. Airborne fungal spores (e.g. Plasmopara viticola / Downy Mildew) and migratory pests (e.g. Spodoptera frugiperda / Fall Armyworm) disperse governed by atmospheric physics and biological thermal kinetics.

15.1 Atmospheric Gaussian Plume Advection Physics

Downwind airborne spore concentration is governed by the steady-state Gaussian plume advection equation:

$$C(x, y, z) = \frac{Q}{2\pi u \sigma_y \sigma_z} \exp\left(-\frac{y^2}{2\sigma_y^2}\right) \left[ \exp\left(-\frac{(z - H)^2}{2\sigma_z^2}\right) + \exp\left(-\frac{(z + H)^2}{2\sigma_z^2}\right) \right]$$

Where:

  • $C(x, y, z)$: Downwind spore concentration at coordinates $(x, y, z)$ in $\text{particles/m}^3$.
  • $Q$: Source release rate (spores/sec) dynamically derived from verified cluster volume.
  • $u$: Mean atmospheric wind velocity at canopy release height ($m/s$).
  • $H$: Effective release height ($H = h_c + \Delta h$).
  • $\sigma_y, \sigma_z$: Pasquill-Gifford lateral and vertical dispersion coefficients expanding as a function of downwind distance $x$: $\sigma_y(x) = a \cdot x^b, \sigma_z(x) = c \cdot x^d$.

15.2 Growing Degree-Day (GDD) Phenological Accumulation

Physical spore transport is coupled with pathogen biology. Insect pupation and fungal sporulation kinetics depend strictly on thermal energy accumulation above a baseline temperature threshold ($T_{base}$):

$$GDD = \max\left(0, \frac{T_{max} + T_{min}}{2} - T_{base}\right)$$

15.3 Multi-Horizon Advection Vector Modeling (24h, 48h, 72h)

For each active outbreak centroid $(x_0, y_0)$, horizontal drift vectors are computed across predictive horizons $t \in \{24, 48, 72\}\text{ hours}$:

$$\Delta x(t) = \sum_{\tau=1}^t u(\tau) \cdot \cos(\theta(\tau)) \cdot \Delta \tau, \quad \Delta y(t) = \sum_{\tau=1}^t u(\tau) \cdot \sin(\theta(\tau)) \cdot \Delta \tau$$

The resulting risk zones are rendered on the surveillance map as directional ellipses stretched along the resultant wind vector, giving extension officers advance intelligence on which downstream talukas to scout before symptoms appear.

16. Statutory Compliance & CIB&RC Chemical Formulations

Chemical pesticide recommendations are never generated by unconstrained language models. All prescribed regimens are strictly governed by active ingredient standards registered with the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC) under the Insecticides Act, 1968.

17. Phased Architecture Roadmap & Production Evolution

Derived directly from our formal engineering product architecture specification, the deployment of KrishiRakshak follows an incremental, four-phase rollout designed to scale safely from a focused agrarian pilot to statewide coverage across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.

🌱 Phase 0 — SIH Prototype (Live Demonstrated Baseline)

Core Focus: Complete offline edge capture $\rightarrow$ two-stage INT8 inference $\rightarrow$ deterministic CIB&RC knapsack dosage calculation $\rightarrow$ basic government surveillance portal.

  • On-device dual-stage INT8 TFLite pipeline (YOLOv8n leaf crop + EfficientNet-Lite0 classifier under 25 ms).
  • WatermelonDB SQLite Schema v4 local vault with client-authoritative UUID v4 and opportunistic background sync.
  • Central PostgreSQL/PostGIS database coordination with automated trg_field_report_triage trigger.
  • Demonstrated with live field scans and calibrated synthetic field datasets across one representative pilot taluka (Nashik).
🍇 Phase 1 — Nashik District Field Pilot (Horticulture & Cash Crop Corridor)

Core Focus: Operational Early Warning System (EWS), automated SMS advisories, and IoT trap integration.

  • Community EWS Deployment: Real-time 10 km spatial outbreak clustering alerting surrounding farm clusters.
  • Automated DLT SMS & Push Advisories: Serving pre-registered regulatory Marathi/Hindi/English templates directly to feature-phone farmers.
  • IoT Pheromone Traps: Solar-powered sensor telemetry ingesting daily insect counts into PostGIS.
  • Sentinel-2 NDVI Telemetry: Ingesting multi-spectral satellite raster bands to monitor vegetative canopy stress.
  • Dedicated Extension Worker App: Equipping field officers with mobile 400% pan-zoom verification queues.
  • Target Milestone: Field AI diagnostic accuracy $\ge 85\%$.
🌾 Phase 2 — Multi-District Regional Scale (Western Maharashtra & Marathwada)

Core Focus: Atmospheric migration forecasting, state command radar, and multilingual speech processing.

  • Atmospheric Migration Forecasting: Numerical Gaussian plume advection coupled with Growing Degree-Day (GDD) phenological accumulation to project airborne pest & spore dispersion across 24h, 48h, and 72h horizons.
  • State Surveillance Dashboard & Cadastral Plot Radar: Sub-meter parcel-level farm visualization (~35m × 35m).
  • Multilingual AI Indic Pipeline: Full speech-to-text and native Indic text-to-speech audio synthesis across dialects.
  • Cloud Scale Optimization: High-speed KD-Tree spatial proximity indexing and binary HEXEWKB decoding.
  • Target Milestones: Field AI accuracy $\ge 92\%$; extension worker query resolution reduced from 24 hours to under 8 hours.
🏛️ Phase 3 — Maharashtra Statewide Rollout (Full Production Platform)

Core Focus: Full statewide deployment across all 36 districts, national AgriStack integration, and continuous learning.

  • 36 Districts Statewide Coverage: Scaled infrastructure supporting 500,000 active smallholder farmers.
  • Full Multispectral Satellite Coverage: Seamless fusion of optical Sentinel-2 imagery with cloud-penetrating Sentinel-1 SAR radar.
  • National Government Integrations: Direct interface with Government of India's AgriStack, Digital Farmer ID, and PM-KISAN registries.
  • Continuous Active Learning Flywheel: Edge-collected extension verifications continuously and cheaply retrain model weights.
  • Macro Agronomic Outcomes: EWS preventive action rate increased from 30% to 60%; aggregate pesticide overuse reduced by 10% to 25%.

17.2 The Five Foundational Architecture Tenets

Every software component, neural tensor, and database trigger in KrishiRakshak is governed by five foundational engineering principles:

  1. Offline-First by Design, Not an Afterthought: Complete diagnostic sovereignty and chemical safety execute locally with zero network dependency.
  2. Hierarchical Intelligence: AI at the farm, early warning intelligence at the community, macro governance decisions at the state level.
  3. Scientific & Statutory Determinism: Pesticide dosages strictly adhere to CIB&RC labels and weather gates, eliminating unconstrained model hallucinations.
  4. Hardware Efficiency over Brute Force: Conserving mobile parameters via deterministic EXIF rules and INT8 quantization enables smooth execution on budget silicon.
  5. Immutable Auditability: Expert overrides never overwrite raw AI inferences, preserving permanent telemetry for continual model evaluation.
